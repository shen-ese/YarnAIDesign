Competitor research

Produce competitor research a client would pay for: evidence-based, illustrated with real screenshots, and ending in recommendations they can act on. This skill exists because ad-hoc competitor research tends to fail in the same ways — competitor lists that miss adjacent players, feature claims that turn out to be wrong, walls of text with no visuals.
